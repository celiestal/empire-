## Why
- **A choppy rebound is the hardest market for every style.**
  - Trend entries get reversed: v4's first 4 trades were all stopped out, and v2's pullback longs were stopped in brief rising hours.
  - Dip-buys get stopped on the swings.
- v3 lost the least because it had few new signals.
- **After 10 days and four market moods** (choppy, steady fall, sideways, crash + rebound), no test is clearly profitable.
  - The only method with a real net gain is v3's hourly trend (tsmom), +$49 since the start, all from the 11-20/21 crash.
