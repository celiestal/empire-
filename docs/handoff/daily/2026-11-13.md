## What was done today
- **Bot** (`lib/paper/scalp/`), fake money only. Four methods:
  - dip-buy (Bollinger + RSI)
  - breakout (range high + volume)
  - trend pullback (EMA)
  - VWAP snap-back
- Timeframes are 1m to 60m. Each method runs buy-only or buy + short.
- **Risk limits:**
  - 0.5% risk per trade, no leverage
  - skips trades whose target or stop is too small compared with fees
  - 2% daily loss stop
  - cool-down after 3 losses in a row
  - 10% kill switch
- Fills are assumed worse than reality, not better.
- **Research** (`scripts/paper/research.ts`):
  - 30 days of real 1-minute Crypto.com prices for BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and DOGE
  - tuned on 10 days across all coins together, then tested on the next 5 days, repeated through the month
  - stress tests: fees 2.5× higher, 3× slippage, cost filters off, top 10% of trades removed, shuffled trade order
- **Live forward test** (`scripts/paper/live.ts`):
  - $10,000 fake money in 25 pots of $400 (5 methods × 5 coins)
  - settings frozen in `lib/paper/scalp/live-config.json`
  - started 2026-11-13 05:17 UTC
- The live loop restarts if the sandbox restarts. Results are replayed from the frozen start, so a gap doesn't change them.
- 15 unit tests pass. Cloud environment `empire-` allows only `api.crypto.com` (public prices). Run Node with `NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1`.

## Key results
**30-day research (out of sample, a falling month: coins -15% to -24%)**
- No method reliably made money. The best were about break-even (profit factor 0.92–1.05).
- The bot lost only about 0–2% per coin while the coins fell much further.
- 1-minute scalping loses at about 0.1% fees. Costs of about 0.24% round trip are bigger than a typical 1-minute move.
- The biggest improvement was the cost filters. They cut losses from about 10–20% per coin to 0–2%.
- The simulator is fair: random trades with zero costs come out flat.

**Live paper test after about 0.7 hours (as of 06:00 UTC)**
- $9,997.61, which is -$2.39 (-0.02%).
- 1 trade: ETH 5m VWAP long, stopped out (-1.2R).
- Buying and holding over the same time: BTC -0.97%, ETH -0.93%, SOL -0.29%, XRP -0.19%, DOGE -0.26%.
- Too early to judge.

**Live bid/ask spreads (median)**
- BTC and ETH: under 0.001%.
- SOL, XRP and DOGE: about 0.01%.
- XRP sometimes reaches 0.08%, above the 0.02% slippage assumption. Watch this.

**Second session's findings** (branch `claude/scalp-bot-daily-summary-fxn5vn`)
- A market-condition detector (rising / falling / sideways / choppy) didn't add an edge over 30 days.
- The market was mostly sideways or choppy. Most losses came from trades opened while prices were falling.
- On 2 years of hourly data, every 1-hour method lost.
- 4-hour breakout (buy-only) was the only clear positive: +3.1% per coin, profit factor 1.20, 10 of 21 periods up, all from rising stretches. The coins fell 30–76% over the same span.
- That 4-hour edge is fragile: it disappears at 0.25% fees or without the top 10% of trades.
- A new trend-following method `tsmom` was also added.

## Strategy changes and how they were tested
- Cost filters (minimum target and minimum stop vs fees): kept. Large improvement on out-of-sample data.
- A filter that stays out when the bigger trend is against the trade: part of the tuning grid, chosen per window.
- Short side: tested. It didn't fix the losses on its own, but it is now in all live methods (the owner is fine with shorting).
- The market-condition detector (other session): not adopted, because it didn't improve out-of-sample results.

## Owner decisions so far
- Approved the plan and the new cloud environment.
- Shorting is fine.
- Wants the bot to read the market and choose the best method itself.
- Real money: possible later, **not yet**. Requirements before a real trial:
  - weeks of clearly positive paper results
  - a separate small account
  - a trade-only key with withdrawals off
  - hard loss limits
  - a weekly profit split, e.g. 70% to holdings and 30% kept for trading
- The bot costs almost nothing to run, so it doesn't need a running-costs pot.
- **No real money or exchange keys** without a separate, explicit decision from the owner.
