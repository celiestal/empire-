## Strategy changes and how they were tested (fresh 30 days, coins -20% to -24%)
- **"Drop pullback from v2": rejected.** In research, v2's 60m pullback is its best method: +2.03% per coin, profit factor 2.63, and it holds up in every stress test. Its 1-for-8 live record is most likely bad luck on a small sample.
- **New v4: hourly trend following (tsmom) + 60m pullback, both only in sideways or rising markets.**
  - 60m tsmom with this filter is the strongest result so far: +4.89% per coin out of sample, profit factor 2.65, profitable on all 5 coins.
  - It still holds up with 2.5× fees, 3× slippage and the best 10% of trades removed.
  - Bad-luck drawdown 4.2%.
  - Caveat: 35 trades over 20 days, and the research windows overlap with last week's.
- v1, v2, 4h and v3 are unchanged. Running tests are never overwritten.
