## Why
- **A sharp, steady fall is what trend following is built for.**
  - v3's hourly trend method (tsmom) shorted the fall and hit 4 of 5 profit targets (+$102).
  - Its breakout method added +$14.
- **Dip-buying lost again in the fall,** as on 11-14. v1's 1m/5m VWAP dip-buys won 10 of 32 (-$33).
- **v2** sat out most of the fall. The 14 trades it did take were entered in brief "sideways" pauses between legs down, and most were stopped out.
- **The filter's trade-off is now clear.** v2, v4 and 4h skip falling markets. That protects them from dip-buy losses in a crash, but it also blocks the trend shorts that profit from one.
