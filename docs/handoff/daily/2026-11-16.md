## Why
- Day 2 (the fall): trend shorts won and dip-buys lost.
- Day 3 (sideways): the reverse.
  - v1's 15m pullback shorts went 0 for 5 (-$11.66).
  - v3's trend shorts went 0 for 4 (-$21.39). They were opened as the fall ended and stopped out as price went sideways.
  - Small reversion trades in sideways markets won a little (+$3.30).
- Every stop-out lost (8 in v1, 4 in v3) and every target won. Losses come from direction, not fees.
- Takeaway: after 3 days and three market moods (choppy, falling, sideways), no single method wins in all of them.
  That's the textbook result, and it's why each test needs weeks across different markets before anything is trusted.
