## Why it won or lost
- Entries while **prices were falling lost**: 2 of 7 won, -$8.26.
  - Shorts into the fall were stopped out by bounces.
  - Dip-buys in a falling market caught falling knives.
- Entries in **sideways markets won**: 4 of 5, +$6.44.
- Losers were full stop-outs (-1.2R). Winners hit targets or time exits.
- The coins move together. Signals often fired on 3 coins at once, so 25 pots are fewer independent bets than they look.
- One day and 23 trades is not enough to prove an edge.

## Strategy changes and how they were tested
- **Skip trades while prices are falling or choppy** (trade only when sideways or rising). Tested on the latest 30 days, out of sample, with stress tests:
  - 60m pullback: profit factor 1.16 → 1.46
  - 60m dip-buy: 0.92 → 1.48
  - 60m VWAP: 0.55 → 1.57
  - 15m VWAP: -1.35% → +0.11% per coin
  - The bad-luck drawdown (shuffled trade order) roughly halved; for 60m VWAP, 10.7% → 2.3%.
  - The 60m setups stay positive at 0.25% fees.
  - Small samples: 12–37 trades per method.
- **Dropped 1–5 minute scalping.** At retail fees the cost filters block almost every trade.
- **4h on 2 years of data:** breakout, long + short, with no new trades in falling markets, came out +3.6% per coin (profit factor 1.24, 10 of 21 periods up), while the coins fell 27–75%. Fragile: it loses money at 0.25% fees.
