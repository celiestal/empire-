## Today's research test: H9, E1 fill-hour robustness
- **Backlog #9 as written was not applicable.** E1 always trades at 00:00 UTC, never in 13–16 UTC. H9 tests the useful question instead: is E1's result an accident of the 00:00 fill?
- **00:00 is never the luckiest hour.** It ranks 6th, 2nd, 6th and 3rd of 6, so the backtest is not flattered by the fill time.
- **Over the full 4-year holdout,** E1 is positive at every fill hour: +41% (00:00) to +64%.
- **But over two-year windows the fill hour moves the result by 19–43 points.** In choppy 2024–26 the 16:00 and 20:00 fills are negative (as low as −12.1%). Execution timing is a large source of luck over short windows, including this paper test.
- **No change is made.** Later fills were better in 2022–24 and worse in 2024–26, so picking an hour now would be curve-fitting. New backlog #11: split each day's trade across several fill hours to average out the timing luck. Details: `hypotheses/H9-fillhour-results.md`.
